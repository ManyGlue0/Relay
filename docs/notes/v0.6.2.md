Relay 0.6.2 lets an interrupted reply carry on.

### New

- **Continue.** A reply you stopped, one that broke off with an error, or one cut off at the length limit now has a **Continue** button next to Copy and Try again. The same model picks up where it stopped, in the same message: no new version, no starting over. Local servers (llama.cpp, Ollama, vLLM and the like) are handed the reply so far and go on from mid-sentence; Claude, OpenAI's API and servers that refuse that get it with a short note asking to go on. Stopped while a tool waited for your OK, or while the model was thinking? Continue runs that step again.

### Fixed

- **A very fast reply no longer stays "streaming".** A reply that finished before Relay had finished sending the message kept its spinner, and the chat looked busy until you switched away.
