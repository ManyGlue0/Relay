Relay 0.5.0 lets your models see, hear and read what you give them.

### New

- **Attach anything.** Use the paperclip, drop files on the chat, or paste a screenshot. Images, audio, video, PDFs, Word and PowerPoint files, code and text all work.
- **See what you attached.** Click any file, in the message box or in the chat, to open it in Relay:
  - images enlarged;
  - videos playing;
  - code and text in a text box, with colors;
  - PDFs page by page.
- **Each model gets what it can take.**
  - Images go as images and audio as audio.
  - Video goes whole to models that watch video. Other models get stills, plus the sound if they hear.
  - PDFs go as files where the server reads them, and as text everywhere else. Scans and other PDFs without text go as their pages, as images. Relay warns you when that's more than 50 pages.
- **Relay knows what each model takes.** It reads this from LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp and OpenRouter, or guesses it from the model's name. Before you send something a model may not take, a small badge warns you. You can correct any model in Settings → Models, under *What each model takes*.
- **Voice messages.** Press the mic, talk, and press it again. A model that hears gets your voice itself, not a transcript.
- **Archive.** Archive a chat from its menu to move it into a folded *Archived* group under the search box. Archived chats still show up in search, and they come back as soon as you write in them.

Attached files stay on your PC, next to your chats, and are removed when their chat is deleted.
